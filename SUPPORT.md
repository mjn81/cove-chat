# Support

- **Setup and configuration:** start with the [documentation](https://covechat.dev/docs/), the [Docker guide](DOCKER.md) and the FAQ.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an [issue](https://github.com/mjn81/cove-chat/issues/new/choose) and fill in the template.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Commercial use:** Cove is free for individuals and small teams. Resale, large organizations and government use need permission. Read the [license](LICENSE) first.

Cove is maintained by one person in their free time, so replies can take a few days.
