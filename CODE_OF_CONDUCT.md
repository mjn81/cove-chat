# Code of conduct

We want Cove to be a welcoming project for everyone.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome advances.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming bad intent.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block participants who break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub.

This is a short statement in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
